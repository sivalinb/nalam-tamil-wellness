# Family review

## Listen with her

- Can she understand the Tamil at its normal speaking pace and volume?
- Does it use her everyday words rather than formal Tamil or unnecessary English?
- Can she replay an answer, understand the one next action and choose “later” comfortably?
- Do lab numbers, food names and important caution phrases sound clear?
- Ask her to explain the action back in her own words. Do not equate successful playback with comprehension.

## Test on her actual iPhone over HTTPS

- Camera / Photos selection, including an HEIC photo; blurred images and failed uploads.
- Microphone permission, a short natural question and correction/replay of the transcript.
- Playback after upload, Safari autoplay fallback and large text accessibility settings.
- Home Screen installation, notification permission and delivery with the phone locked.
- Tap-to-open reminder, quiet hours, timezone, “done” and “later” persistence.
- A weak connection, no connection and a server/API failure.

## Review health content with a clinician

- Confirm that her actual cholesterol findings and medical context are explained accurately.
- Verify that exercise examples fit her mobility, pain, fall risk and any restrictions.
- Check food suggestions against her conditions, allergies and dietary needs.
- Record the clinician's treatment and follow-up instructions. The AI does not make those decisions.
- Check urgent-symptom messaging and avoid treating the app as medical triage.

## Badaga later

- Have a fluent speaker translate and record each common phrase with explicit consent.
- Independently review the meaning, pronunciation and normal speaking tone.
- Begin with fixed phrases and recorded audio. Do not infer free-form language support from one successful translation.
- Evaluate speech recognition on real speakers and accents before adding voice questions.
- Have medical explanations independently checked before enabling dynamic Badaga report speech.
