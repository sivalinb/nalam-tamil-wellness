Caregiver view

Confirm her report, then help her take one small step.

Confirm every number and unit against the photo. Confirmation checks transcription; it does not replace clinician review.

Gentle reminders

iPhone push reminders need HTTPS, iOS 16.4+, installation on the Home Screen and notification permission. Tap a reminder to hear the prompt. The Python server must stay running.

Build a Badaga voice pack

Start with short recordings made and reviewed by a fluent Badaga speaker. The app uses these for daily prompts. Free-form report explanations continue in Tamil until Badaga has been independently validated.

Her information, her choice

Photos and voice questions are sent to OpenAI for processing. API data is not used for model training by default, but provider abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days. This app uses store=false for text responses.

Health records, pending photos, saved answers, audio and push subscriptions are encrypted locally. A report photo is deleted from the active record after you confirm the numbers. Voice recordings are processed without saving the input recording. No advertising or analytics.
